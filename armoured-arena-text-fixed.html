Match

  • R — new map rematch
  • Pick your rules: Standard, Mirror (both sides in the same tank — copied or drawn at random every round) or Weight class (both sides restricted to a weight band).
  • Stats come from your blueprint: mass, engine power, armour, guns.
  • Four tanks are built in from the start — open the tank pool and battle without any uploads.
  • Armour is real: shells that can't punch the plate they strike do 25% damage.
  • Tanks collide — rams shove the lighter tank around and cost speed.
